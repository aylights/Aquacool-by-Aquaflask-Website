About this campaign

Campaign notes for the instructor (in English).

The product

How the Aquacool cooling system works

Removable gel pockets give direct cooling to the upper back, PCM panels absorb extra heat around the chest, sides and back, and rear zipper vents let warm, humid air escape.

Layer 1Cowhide leather shell with adjustable zipper vents
Layer 2PCM thermal-regulation textile that absorbs excess heat
Layer 3Removable pre-cooled gel pockets for localized cooling
Layer 4Moisture-wicking PET mesh lining, breathable against skin
Execution

Digital marketing: "Build Your Jacket"

An interactive builder where riders design their Aquacool jacket in Aquaflask colors, find their size, match it to their bottle, and reserve it as a bundle.

Message

"We keep your drinks cool. Now it's your turn."

Aquaflask already owns "cold" in people's minds. Aquacool stretches that promise from the bottle to the rider: stay cool, stay fresh.

Target market

Gen X and Millennial urban riders

Riders who need a jacket for sun and road protection but hate how hot and sticky regular riding jackets get in Philippine traffic. Classic dark builds come first for riders who want a traditional look.

Rationale

Why a builder fits Aquacool

  • Leverages the parent brand: Aquaflask is known for affordable quality and lifestyle colors, so picking those colors makes the extension feel familiar right away.
  • Works for every rider: classic dark builds suit riders who want a traditional jacket, bright builds let younger riders show personality, and the quiz recommends a build from riding habits and style.
  • Speaks the rider's language: an English/Filipino toggle makes the page comfortable for more of the target market.
  • Removes buying doubts: the size guide answers the biggest worry about buying riding gear online, and the night view shows the reflective trim that keeps riders visible after work.
  • Explains the technology: each part describes its feature as you select it, so riders learn about the PCM panels and gel pockets while they design.
  • Proves the benefit: the "See the difference" slider shows the cooling benefit visually instead of asking riders to take it on faith.
  • Drives the bundle: three bundle tiers with visible savings nudge riders from jacket-only to jacket + Aquaflask, and the top tier adds spare gel packs, which starts the retention habit.
  • Creates shareable content: the downloadable build card and "Copy to share" caption turn customers into word-of-mouth promoters in rider groups, with #StayCoolStayFresh.
Where it fits in the customer journey
  1. Awareness: "Beyond the Bottle" teaser ads point to the builder.
  2. Introduction: rider influencers share their own build cards.
  3. Trial: rider community events let people try on the jacket they designed.
  4. Conversion: pre-orders and jacket + Aquaflask bundles are reserved through the builder.
  5. Retention: replacement gel pockets are offered in the same colors as the rider's build.
